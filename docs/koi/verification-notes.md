## 1. VAT registration threshold: unresolved

| Source | Claim |
|---|---|
| [Kintsugi Bangladesh VAT guide](https://trykintsugi.com/sales-tax-guides/apac/bangladesh) | Mandatory registration above BDT 50 lakh annual turnover |
| [Lappa VAT guide](https://lappa.org/guides/vat/bangladesh-vat-registration-and-compliance-guide/) | BDT 50 lakh; one secondary source says the figure was revised by ordinance in January 2025 |
| [TRW Law, common VAT mistakes](https://trw.org/common-vat-mistakes-bangladesh/) | Under Finance Act 2026, threshold remains above BDT 3 crore |
| Same sources | Software and IT service providers may be registrable regardless of turnover (one source only) |

Standard VAT rate of 15% is consistent across sources.

**Action:** check the Finance Act 2026 text or an NBR notice, or ask a Bangladeshi chartered accountant.

## 2. Restraint of trade and outside employment

- Contract Act 1872, section 27 (applies in Bangladesh). [Daily Star, 2011](https://archive.thedailystar.net/law/2011/03/01/advocate.htm) reports that agreements restraining lawful business are void, and that a restraint cannot extend beyond the employment period (exception: sale of goodwill).
- Restraints during employment are treated differently. This comes from Indian authority, which shares the statute. No Bangladeshi judgment was found.
- Confidentiality and IP obligations are separate and may survive employment.

**Action:** a Bangladeshi lawyer should read the employment contract's outside-business, conflict-of-interest, confidentiality and IP clauses.
